# Extreme Privacy Website Outline

## 1. Website Purpose

The Extreme Privacy website should position the company as a **premium construction and building-performance specialist** for privacy-oriented residential and commercial properties. The site should establish credibility with affluent property owners, architects, estate managers, security professionals, family offices, and authorized AI agents seeking information or quote pathways.

The website should present Extreme Privacy as a specialized division of **Robert Martin Koehler Contractor Business**. Its core promise is not medical protection or absolute invisibility. Its promise is **discreet construction, measurable electromagnetic attenuation, privacy-oriented building design, secure-room planning, and documented commissioning**.

The primary website goals are to:

1. Generate qualified assessment requests.
2. Convert high-value property owners into design and construction consultations.
3. Provide architects, security firms, estate managers, and AI agents with structured service information.
4. Explain what the company can measure, build, and maintain.
5. Establish a defensible brand through careful technical and claims language.

## 3. Global Brand and Design Direction

The visual identity should communicate **quiet authority, architectural quality, discretion, and technical precision**. It should avoid the visual language of conspiracy, fear marketing, or consumer gadget sales.

The recommended palette is deep navy, mineral off-white, architectural teal, and a restrained warm-gold accent. Photography should emphasize high-end architectural details, doors, glazing, building envelopes, technical drawings, secure meeting environments, and material craftsmanship. Avoid generic padlocks, hooded figures, surveillance-camera montages, glowing “radiation” graphics, or images that imply medical treatment.

Navigation should remain concise. The primary header call to action should be **Request a Confidential Assessment**. A secondary action should be **Request Information or a Quote**. The footer should identify the parent company: **Extreme Privacy, a specialized division of Robert Martin Koehler Contractor Business**.

## 5. Services Page

The Services page should provide a high-level overview and route visitors to more specific pages. Each service should include the client problem, what Extreme Privacy delivers, typical project stage, expected deliverables, and an appropriate CTA.

### 5.1 Privacy and electromagnetic assessment

Explain that the assessment reviews construction type, openings, glazing, doors, penetrations, wireless systems, adjacent signal sources, and client priorities. The output should include a baseline assessment, risk register, preliminary options, constraints, and a budget range.

**CTA:** Book an Assessment

### 5.2 Design and engineering coordination

Explain coordination with architects, engineers, low-voltage specialists, security professionals, and permitting teams. Emphasize that the entire enclosure matters, including seams, windows, doors, HVAC, cabling, grounding, and maintenance access.

**CTA:** Discuss a Design Scope

### 5.3 Residential construction and renovation

Describe privacy rooms, home offices, controlled communications areas, new-build packages, and discreet renovations. Stress that the goal is to preserve architectural quality and livability.

**CTA:** Explore Residential Projects

### 5.4 Commercial and professional spaces

Describe secure conference rooms, confidential meeting spaces, protected records areas, controlled wireless zones, and commercial privacy programs. State clearly that building performance complements cybersecurity and operational security rather than replacing them.

**CTA:** Discuss a Commercial Project

### 5.5 Testing and commissioning

Explain pre-installation review, in-progress inspection, post-installation testing, documentation, and corrective work. Include a sample list of report contents: test locations, target frequencies, equipment, calibration status, environmental conditions, results, limitations, and maintenance recommendations.

**CTA:** Request a Testing Consultation

### 5.6 Maintenance and retesting

Explain why doors, seals, penetrations, wireless systems, and later renovations can affect performance. Offer annual or biannual inspection and retesting packages.

**CTA:** Plan Ongoing Maintenance

## 8. SCIF-Capable Planning Page

This page must be precise and conservative. It should not imply that Extreme Privacy independently accredits SCIFs or can guarantee government acceptance.

### Recommended headline

> Construction support for SCIF-capable projects, coordinated with the client’s sponsor and accrediting authorities.

### Core copy

> Extreme Privacy can support planning and construction for projects intended to meet Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility requirements. Final criteria, construction details, operating procedures, and accreditation decisions are controlled by the applicable government sponsor, Accrediting Official, and technical authorities.

### Functional planning categories

Describe the following as planning categories rather than universal mandatory rooms:

- Secure perimeter and controlled entry
- Secure working areas
- Secure conference or briefing room
- Communications and protected-distribution zone
- Equipment or technical-support room
- Storage or records area where approved
- Construction-security and secure-material staging
- Life-safety and building-services spaces

### Accreditation disclaimer

> A project is not a SCIF merely because it contains shielding, secure doors, or a restricted room. SCI may only be processed, stored, used, or discussed in a facility accredited by the appropriate authority. Extreme Privacy provides construction and coordination support; it does not replace the sponsor, Accrediting Official, security authority, or current governing standards.

**CTA:** Begin a Qualified SCIF-Capable Inquiry

## 9. Agent-Ready Quote Intake Page

This is a differentiating page for AI agents, procurement systems, architects’ assistants, estate-management platforms, and professional referral tools.

### Recommended headline

> A structured communications channel for authorized AI agents seeking information and quotes.

### Supporting copy

> Extreme Privacy provides approved service information, qualification pathways, budgetary ranges, and quote-intake options for authorized AI agents. Binding proposals require scope validation and human approval.

### Capabilities to describe

- Searchable service descriptions
- Geographic coverage and project categories
- Starting price ranges and assumptions
- Required information for an assessment
- Structured quote-request intake
- Lead and reference-number creation
- Human review before binding proposals
- Version-controlled service and pricing information

### Suggested agent workflow

1. Identify property type, location, project stage, privacy objective, budget, and timeline.
2. Return the relevant approved service description and limitations.
3. Create an inquiry or paid-assessment request.
4. Return a non-binding budget range when the assumptions are adequate.
5. Route the request to a qualified human for scope validation and proposal approval.

### Security and privacy controls

The page should state that unauthenticated agents must not submit sensitive property details, security-sensitive information, personal data, classified information, or credentials. The system should apply authentication, rate limits, request logging, version control, and human approval for contractual commitments.

**CTAs:** Request API or Partner Information; Submit a Quote Request

## 10. Process Page

Use a six-stage timeline:

1. **Qualify:** Confirm objective, budget, property, lawful use, and decision-makers.
2. **Assess:** Document baseline conditions, constraints, and privacy risks.
3. **Design:** Specify frequency bands, assemblies, systems, and test criteria.
4. **Build:** Execute construction with material records, inspection checkpoints, and penetration controls.
5. **Commission:** Test defined locations and frequencies; document results and limitations.
6. **Maintain:** Inspect, retest, and control changes after occupancy or renovation.

Each stage should include a short description and a clear handoff. The process page should make the company feel organized and low-risk to a sophisticated buyer.

## 11. Claims and Technical Education Page

This page can be called **What We Measure** or **Understanding Building Performance**. It should educate without making medical claims.

### Topics

- What electromagnetic attenuation means
- Why frequency range matters
- Why doors, windows, seams, and penetrations matter
- Difference between material test data and installed-building performance
- Why field conditions affect results
- Difference between privacy-risk reduction and absolute prevention
- Relationship among physical privacy, cybersecurity, access control, and operational security
- Why maintenance and retesting are necessary

### Approved language examples

- “Frequency-specific electromagnetic attenuation measured at defined locations.”
- “Privacy-oriented building design and reduced signal leakage within defined bands.”
- “Designed to reduce certain sensing opportunities; performance depends on frequency, geometry, distance, equipment, and conditions.”
- “Independent testing and documented commissioning.”

### Language to avoid

- “Blocks all radiation.”
- “Prevents cancer or illness.”
- “Makes the property invisible.”
- “Stops every surveillance method.”
- “Guaranteed protection” without a defined test protocol and remedy.

## 14. Lead-Intake Forms

### Confidential assessment form

Collect only what is needed for initial qualification:

- Name and preferred contact method
- Organization or property role
- Property city and state
- Residential or commercial project
- New build, renovation, retrofit, or advisory request
- Desired room or building function
- Desired timeline
- Budget range
- Whether an architect, engineer, security consultant, or estate manager is already involved
- Preferred next step

Do not request classified information, detailed threat intelligence, sensitive floor plans, credentials, or unnecessary personal data through the public form.

### Quote-intake form for authorized agents and partners

Use structured fields for property type, location, service category, approximate square footage, project phase, target completion date, desired test criteria, and referral organization. Clearly label all budgetary figures as non-binding until scope and site conditions are validated.

## 16. Analytics and Success Metrics

Track a small number of meaningful measures:

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Qualified assessment requests | Measures demand quality rather than raw traffic. |
| Assessment-to-proposal conversion | Tests whether paid discovery is effective. |
| Proposal win rate | Measures positioning and pricing. |
| Average project value | Tracks premium-market performance. |
| Gross margin by service type | Separates construction margin from specialist and recurring revenue. |
| Referral-source conversion | Identifies high-value partners. |
| Agent-originated inquiries | Measures the agent-ready channel. |
| Quote-request completion rate | Identifies friction in structured intake. |
| Commissioning pass or correction rate | Measures delivery quality. |
| Maintenance attachment rate | Measures recurring-revenue potential. |

The site should not optimize for page views alone. A small number of qualified assessments can be more valuable than high-volume consumer traffic.

## 17. Recommended Launch Sequence

### Phase 1: Credibility foundation

Launch the Home, Services, About, Process, Claims and Technical Education, and Contact pages. Include the parent-company relationship and a confidential assessment CTA.

### Phase 2: Segment expansion

Add Residential Privacy, Commercial and Secure Spaces, SCIF-Capable Planning, and sample deliverables. Publish the first three technical articles.

### Phase 3: Agent-ready intake

Add the Agent-Ready Quote Intake page, structured forms, approved service data, authentication, request logging, and human review workflows. Begin with a controlled partner pilot before publishing broad API access.

### Phase 4: Proof and scale

Publish redacted case studies, verified test summaries, partner references, maintenance plans, and geographic expansion information after the company has completed representative projects.
